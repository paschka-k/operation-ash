# Release notes

What changed in OPERATION ASH with every release, in plain words, newest first. The developer's list is in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).
This file is generated by `npm run notes` from `src/core/releases.js`; edit the data, not the file.

## 1.5.0: Wave 15: ten maps and new vehicles

2026-11-20, this release

- Ten maps, one per wave: City Block, Port, Industrial Zone, Old Town, Railyard, Airfield, Winter Village, Downtown at Night, Desert Village and Construction Site. The name shows in the wave banner.
- From wave 6 you pick the next map yourself: the shop between waves shows cards with a view from above. In endless mode the choice is always open.
- Each map has its own light and weather: snow in the winter village, dust in the desert, rain on the construction site; the street lamps of the night map really light the ground.
- The big map and the radar draw the view from above of the map you are on.
- Cars and buses on the streets are much more detailed: modern electric cars, trucks and buses in several colours stand and burn on the roads.
- The scooters, skateboards and motorbikes of the riders are more detailed too.
- Block look: the player has a shadow, sandbags look like sandbags, facades gained window reveals, floor bands and parapets, and the haze against the sun is thinner.
- When the game is started with the launcher (`npm run play` or `play-windows.cmd`), the graphics card can be chosen in Settings. In an ordinary tab the browser chooses it; the game only shows which card it draws on.
- A "What's new" window in the main menu, and the version number.

Known issues:

- The first switch to the old town or the night map takes 1.2 to 2.5 s behind the loading screen; later switches are faster.
- The night map is rather dark; it and the old town are the heaviest maps (about 5% more draw calls).
- Car windows are opaque and the inside shows only on open and burnt cars; some models are longer than their collision box and stick out up to a quarter of a metre.
- The shop with ten map cards is taller than a 720 px window, so it scrolls, and the map schemes finish drawing within about a second.
- Restarting the launcher on another graphics card asks for confirmation in a browser dialog. The launcher without Chrome and Edge, a busy profile, sound by ear and a real gamepad were not tried.
- Rain, snow and dust also fall inside buildings: the weather ignores roofs.
- On "Port" there is an invisible wall over the sea at the edge of the quay: a grenade thrown into the sea bounces back.
- The "What's new" window is taller than the screen: it scrolls with the mouse and keyboard but not with a gamepad.

## 1.4.1: Fixes after wave 14

2026-11-20, commit e3e6751

- Riders turn their body towards you and fire when the barrel points at you, not sideways.
- Riders steer round wrecks and abandoned vehicles instead of dismounting one after another.
- Motorbikes hold the road better in turns. A bike moving off close to you used to stop behind you; now it pushes you and deals a share of the ramming damage.
- The wave banner sits between the radar and the kill feed and no longer covers the big map.
- On a gamepad the shop is walked down the weapon column; LT and RT release a locked sprint.
- Weapon names: the HR-12 Battle Rifle and the AR-7 Assault Rifle are no longer confused.

Known issues:

- A motorbike standing right behind you (closer than about 0.4 m) still brakes: it has no room to pick up speed for a push.
- When swerving, a rider casts its rays only at walls and objects: it waits behind a bot on foot in its lane instead of going round. Where there is no room on either side it dismounts after 3 s, as before.
- Fragile but working: the HUD draws the riders' marks on the big map with a copy of its layout; the commander's grenade spares the bots by swapping the timers of `props`; the tests of the rider's sub-steps and reload check less than their names say.

## 1.3.0: Waves 11 to 13: controls, economy, weapons

2026-11-15, commit a41ba9c

- Sprint stamina: you can run for 3 seconds, then it recovers; a bar above the health.
- A larger radar, a big map on the M key and an "enemies on the map" setting.
- Every key can be rebound; the game works with a gamepad, menus and the shop included.
- Money for kills and waves, and a shop between waves without a timer: weapons, upgrades, armour, grenades and ammo refills.
- Revival on the wave you died on.
- Six new weapons: an SMG, a battle rifle, a sniper rifle, a machine gun, a minigun and a flamethrower.
- Hand grenades.

Known issues:

- Not tried: a real gamepad, a real mouse capture, sound by ear, opening `dist/game.html` with a double click (`file://`).

- Two weapons are called "Автомат" in Russian (AR-7 and HR-12), and both "Rifle" in English.
- The sniper rifle kills with one body hit only in the first waves: the health of the enemies grows.
- The game may draw on the integrated graphics although the system has a discrete card (Intel Iris Xe next to an RTX 4070, measured on 14 November): the Windows "Graphics" setting for the browser switches it, and the player has to make it.

## 1.2.0: Waves 9 and 10: weapons in hand and photoscans

2026-11-14, commit b07b1ea

- Poly Haven photoscans (CC0): eight surfaces and five props fitted to the level; walls, road and crates are closer to a photograph. They switch off in Settings or with `?noscans`.
- The weapons in hand are sculpted again: a pistol with a moving slide, a rifle with a sight, a shotgun; gloves with fingers and sleeves.
- The light on the weapon follows the world, not the camera.
- Crates, barrels and pickups are cheaper to draw with the same objects on screen.
- Craters on the road are soot stains, road markings and paper are toned down.

Known issues:

- The full frame time with scans was not measured (the browser pane was hidden); the scans need about three times more video memory: some 40 textures of 1024 × 1024 instead of generated ones.
- Buildings, weapons and soldiers are still made of simple shapes: walls, road and props are closer to a photograph, but this is not Unreal Engine 5.
- Left from the blind acceptance report: a city of boxes with a grid of windows, flat one-colour blocks (containers, signs), sandbags that look like bricks, a milky haze against the sun, black window openings without frames or glass, rubble without contact shadows, dark flat light inside the buildings, no shadow of the player or the weapon.
- The weapons are still generated by code, not sculpted or scanned; in the usual position the right hand and the trigger are hidden by the body of the weapon.
- Not tried: opening the built files with a double click (`file://`, `dist/game-scans.html` included), a real mouse capture and sound by ear.

## 1.1.0: Fewer draw calls, a new soldier, breaches

- The weapon in the player's hands is made of 68 meshes.
- The muzzle in a bot's shot is a formula, not a bone: the tracer of a crouching bot starts up to 0.28 m above the visible barrel.
- Sound by ear and opening `dist/game.html` with a double click (`file://`) were not tried.

## 1.0.0: First release: OPERATION ASH

2026-11-13, commit 9b3cac5

- A first-person shooter in a ruined 90 by 90 m city block: ten buildings furnished inside, streets, barricades, containers and a sniper perch on a container roof.
- Three weapons: a pistol, a rifle and a shotgun. Ten waves of bots, then an endless mode.
- Bots take cover, peek out, and warn of a burst with a laser and a sound; three difficulty tiers.
- Health regenerates after five quiet seconds; crates break, barrels explode, medkits and ammo boxes come back.
- Three quality presets with automatic downgrade, settings, Russian and English, sound.
- The game opens as one file, `dist/game.html`, with nothing to install.

Known issues:

- No photoscans yet: downloading them waits for the owner's consent; the scripts are ready.
- The sound was not checked by ear.
- Opening `dist/game.html` with a double click (`file://`) was not tried: the browser pane cannot open it.
